About

The Band

London-based post-punk and alternative rock four-piece, stylised as sounds mint.

sounds mint was conceived at the end of 2021 and officially born in 2022, but it was something that felt like years in the making.

Having been in previous bands in their late teens, Kelvin (frontman) and Rudy (drums) did the only thing they knew how: keep creating music and start a new band. Turns out everything happened for a greater good. They have finally found their voice, coupled with a desperation to share a message with the world, and that is what keeps sounds mint pressing forwards and upwards.

Having grown up on bands such as Rage Against The Machine, Nirvana and Joy Division, existential angst and an honest reflection of life and London living are central themes to the music. Nothing is sugar-coated and nothing is held back, with hard-hitting lyrics and catchy, honest hooks. This is both to honour themselves and to honour all the true musicians who came before them and inspired them to do something meaningful and relatable.

Linking up with Robbie (lead guitar) and Layla (bass) in 2022, the band have taken big strides musically. Having met some amazing musicians along the way, they have turned their DIY bedroom post-punk outcries into a full-on four-piece monster that has been blowing people away all around Europe with intense live shows, including supporting Blur on UK and European tour dates.

Since then: the Club Penguin EP in 2024, the One Day We’ll Make It Out Of Here, ’Til Then; EP in 2025, and Milk It, the mixtape, in 2026.

Community sits at the centre of it all. Around the “Tin” release they hosted a football tournament in Shoreditch with artists including bby and Peter Xan, and teamed up with City Harvest London, Brixton Soup Kitchen and Feast With Us UK to raise support for London food charities.

Where the name came from

Two mint basses

During an early session with their friend Amin, he and Kelvin both turned up with seafoam-green bass guitars, so the band became MINT.

Then a band from the north of England sent a cease and desist, with a battle of the bands threatened to settle who kept the name.

Their Instagram was already @soundsmint, and “sounds mint” is northern slang for something being good. They kept that instead.
